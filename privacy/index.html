Nothing leaves your device

That is not a slogan. This page is the whole of it, and it is short because there is very little to say.

no account, ever

There is no sign-up, no login, and no password. You are not a user record anywhere, because there is nowhere for a user record to be — this is a set of static files with no server and no database behind it.

what is stored, and where

Anything the piece remembers about you — the avatar you shaped, the runs you have taken — is written to your own browser's storage on your own device, under keys beginning secondself:. It is never transmitted anywhere. Clearing your browser data erases it, and so does the button below.

There are no cookies. Nothing here reads your screen-time data, your contacts, your location, your camera, or your microphone, and nothing here asks for a permission.

what is counted

One thing: how many people opened the page. It is counted with GoatCounter, which sets no cookies, collects no personal data, and builds no profile of you across sites. It records a page view and the site that referred you. That is the entire dataset.

If your browser sends Do Not Track or a Global Privacy Control signal, no request is made at all. There is no advertising, no third-party tracker, no fingerprinting, and nothing is sold or shared — there is no one to sell it to.

You will not find that number displayed anywhere inside the experience. A visible engagement metric, on a piece about what engagement metrics do to people, would be a joke at your expense.

forget me

This erases everything Second Self has stored on this device, and nothing else.
